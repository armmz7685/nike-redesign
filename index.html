THE SECOND LIFE PROJECT

Good shoes.
More stories.

Style should not have to start with a new pair.

Footwear takes materials and energy to produce. Discarding a wearable pair simply because its appearance has changed wastes its remaining useful life. RE:DESIGN proposes a different approach: keep the shoes you own, repair what needs attention and refresh their look only where necessary.

Designed for sneaker lovers and young adults, our proposed service combines personal expression with longer use. Repair comes first; recycling is the last resort.

MATERIAL MATTERS

Recycled laces

When laces need replacing, consider recycled polyester instead of virgin fibre. Keep usable laces in service.

Consider compatible low-VOC coatings for targeted colour restoration. Check durability and product safety before use.

Recycled rubber

Where a sole is beyond repair, investigate compatible recycled-content replacements that meet grip and durability needs.

Reuse the original box where possible, or choose recycled cardboard without unnecessary decorative packaging.

    KEEP THEM GOING

    Care is a habit.

    Simple habits can help keep footwear usable for longer.

    Clean gently and regularly

    Remove loose dirt with a soft brush. Use a suitable cleaner for the material and avoid soaking the shoe.

    Dry naturally

    Air-dry away from direct heat. Store in a dry, ventilated place.

    Repair before replacing

    Check worn laces, loose stitching and sole damage. Seek a suitable repair service when needed.

    Can every shoe be recycled?

    No. Mixed materials and local facilities affect recyclability. Check accepted footwear with your local collection provider.
